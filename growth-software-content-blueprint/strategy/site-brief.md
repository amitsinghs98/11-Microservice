# Site Brief

Input for the `seo-content-writer` workflow. **Derived from the owner's strategy documents** (the 100-article DOCX, the additional-200 map and the two build prompts), not from crawling a live site, because no site or domain exists yet. Fields the sources don't supply are marked **Not supplied**. Never fill them with invented details.

## Business

- **Brand:** InsightToolz (set 7 November 2026).
- **Domain:** https://insighttoolz.com (set 7 November 2026).
- **What it is:** An independent growth-software discovery and comparison publication. It is editorial, not a review marketplace.
- **Positioning (from the DOCX):** "An independent growth-software discovery platform that helps agencies and lean business teams compare the tools behind organic acquisition, content production, outreach, CRM, automation, and measurement—using use-case fit, clear pricing context, and honest limitations."
- **Core promise:** Help buyers choose growth software through clear use-case comparisons, real pricing context, practical trade-offs and independent editorial judgment.

## Reader

1. Small SEO and digital marketing agencies managing several client accounts.
2. Founders and operators buying their first sales, marketing and analytics stack.
3. In-house B2B marketing teams that have to justify software costs.
4. Ecommerce growth teams choosing email, social, conversion and merchandising software.
5. Product and growth teams at SaaS companies (clusters 9, 12 and 15).

They are busy, numerate and sceptical of vendor marketing. They want a shortlist and the reasons behind it, not a tour of the category.

## Voice

- Second person ("you"), with contractions, plain English and short paragraphs (2–4 sentences).
- The tone of an experienced operator advising a peer: specific, candid about trade-offs, no hype, no humour for its own sake.
- Sentence length is mixed, averaging around 15–20 words. Lead with the answer.
- Use British or US spelling consistently within a page. The corpus default is US spelling.

### Say / don't say

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Our editorial assessment" | "We tested" / "our testing found" (no testing was done) |
| "Based on the product information reviewed" | "Users rate…" / "verified customer rating" |
| "A good fit for teams that…" | "Best-in-class", "game-changing", "revolutionary", "ultimate solution" |
| "Consider another option if…" | "Number one" / "best overall" without the stated criteria next to it |
| "Pricing checked on 4 November 2026" | Any price that wasn't fetched from the official page |
| "The vendor's pricing page lists…" | "In today's digital landscape…" or other generic openers |
| "Skip it if…" | Equal praise for every product |

### Words from the business's own mouth (echo these)

- "What it really costs"
- "Best for / not for"
- "What we checked"
- "Price checked on"
- "Correct this page"
- "Use-case fit, clear pricing context, and honest limitations"

## Proof available

- Official vendor pricing pages, documentation and release notes, fetched and dated.
- **No** hands-on testing, customer data, case studies, surveys, benchmarks, traffic figures or reviews. None of these may be implied.

## Author

Byline: **InsightToolz Editorial Team** (set 7 November 2026 in `site.config.json` → `author`). No fictional person, bio or credentials may be invented. Replace it with a real reviewer's name and bio when one exists.

## First-hand experience

**Not supplied** for any article. Every article carries the literal marker `[YOUR EXPERIENCE HERE — the skill will not invent this]` in a dedicated section, for a human with real experience to fill before publication.

## Call to action

- Soft CTA, placed after the value has been delivered: point the reader to the next most useful page (a comparison, pricing guide or how-to guide) and to the vendor's official site to start a trial.
- No affiliate links, no "book a demo with us" (the publisher sells nothing yet), and no sponsored modules.

## Hard rules

1. Never fabricate prices, features, limits, integrations, quotes, review counts, ratings, tests, performance lifts, affiliate terms, commissions, vendor relationships or search volumes.
2. Never copy wording from G2, DeployHyre, SaasHyre, MakeToolz or any other publisher.
3. Paid placement never changes order, assessment or inclusion.
4. Every pricing claim carries a link to the official source and a checked-on date.
5. Primary queries are search-intent hypotheses. No search volumes are known or stated.
