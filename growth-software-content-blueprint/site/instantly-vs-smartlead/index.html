Should you choose Instantly or Smartlead?

Choose Smartlead if you're an agency that needs to separate clients at a published per-client price. Choose Instantly if you want sending, lead data and AI agents from one vendor and you'll run on its Hypergrowth tier or above anyway.

  • Solo sender, a few thousand emails a month: entry plans are close. Smartlead Base is cheaper and stores more contacts but has no CRM access, API or webhooks; Instantly Growth only previews replies. Decide on whether you'll reply inside the tool.
  • Agency running separate client programs: Smartlead prices white-label client workspaces individually and includes three on its top tier, so you can forecast cost per client. Instantly's pricing page doesn't price client separation, so get a written quote.
  • Team that also buys lead data: Instantly sells a separate credits subscription for its Lead Finder database, enrichment and AI agents. Smartlead bundles a verified prospect email allowance that is an add-on on its lower tiers.
  • High-volume sender: both sell dedicated sending infrastructure at upper tiers, and neither changes your obligations on consent and opt-outs.

In our editorial assessment, neither tool is cheaper in every case; client count, mailbox count and entry-tier webhooks move the answer.

How are Instantly and Smartlead positioned?

Instantly sells a modular system: Outreach plans for sending and warm-up, plus a separate Credits product for lead data and AI agents. Smartlead prices on send volume and stored contacts, and builds its agency pitch around paid client workspaces and infrastructure add-ons.

On Instantly's pricing page, Outreach plans cover sending, warm-up and the Unibox reply inbox, while Instantly Credits pay for database lookups, enrichment and AI agents (checked 4 November 2026). Its built-in CRM comes with Hypergrowth and above rather than as a separate purchase.

Smartlead's pricing page says every plan includes unlimited email accounts and sells add-ons for white-label client workspaces, dedicated servers and verification credits (checked 5 November 2026). Both sit in the sending-first group of our cold email software shortlist for B2B agencies; if reps also work calls and LinkedIn, compare sales engagement platforms for SDR teams.

What do Instantly and Smartlead really cost for a solo sender and an agency?

For one person sending about 4,000 emails a month, the modeled totals are roughly $81 on Instantly and $69 on Smartlead. For a five-client agency, Smartlead comes to about $531 a month with published workspace fees, while Instantly's mailboxes and domains alone come to about $325 before the Hypergrowth subscription and any quoted client separation.

Both scenarios are hypothetical. They use the list prices above, monthly billing, US dollars and no promotions, and assume about 30 cold emails per mailbox per working day over 22 working days (our planning assumption, not a vendor rule).

Scenario A: what does one-person outbound cost?

Assumptions: 3 domains, 6 fresh Google mailboxes, about 4,000 sends and fewer than 1,000 new contacts a month, with lead data sourced elsewhere.

  • Instantly: Growth $47 + six done-for-you mailboxes at $5 ($30) + three domains at $15/year (about $3.75 a month) = about $81 a month.
  • Smartlead: Base $39 + six fresh mailboxes at $4.50 ($27) + three domains at $13/year (about $3.25 a month) = about $69 a month.

The $12 gap matters less than the feature gap. If you need in-app replies or CRM handoff, compare Instantly Hypergrowth (confirm its current price) with Smartlead Pro at $94.

Scenario B: what does a five-client agency pay?

Assumptions: 5 client programs kept separate, 20 domains, 60 mailboxes, about 40,000 sends and 15,000 new contacts a month, with webhooks needed for CRM handoff.

  • Instantly: 60 done-for-you mailboxes ($300) + 20 domains (about $25 a month) = about $325 a month, plus the Hypergrowth subscription and client separation, neither of which we could price from a consistent official figure.
  • Smartlead: Pro $94 + five workspaces at $29 ($145) + 60 fresh mailboxes ($270) + 20 domains (about $22 a month) = about $531 a month.

Mailboxes cost more than either platform here, the usual pattern at agency scale. Verification comes on top; Smartlead's credit tiers showed different prices across its official pages, so price them at checkout. How do warm-up, replies, data and CRM handoff differ day to day?

The biggest daily difference is reply handling: Instantly keeps full in-app replies and webhooks for Hypergrowth, while Smartlead keeps CRM access and webhooks for Pro. Warm-up and dedicated infrastructure exist on both, but the stronger options sit higher up each price list.

Warm-up and infrastructure

Both include automated warm-up. Smartlead's plan descriptions tier the warm-up pool: Foundation on Base, Growth on Pro and Premium on the Unlimited plans (checked 5 November 2026). Instantly's server and IP rotation system (SISR) switches on automatically for Hypergrowth, Light Speed and Enterprise workspaces, with dedicated private IP blocks tied to Light Speed (checked 5 November 2026). Smartlead has renamed SmartServers as SmartInfra, with a single dedicated-IP option at $39 a month (checked 5 November 2026). For an independent check, add a monitor from our email deliverability tools comparison.

Inbox management

On Instantly Growth, the Unibox is preview-only and you reply from Gmail or Outlook; full replying starts at Hypergrowth (checked 5 November 2026). That gate is the likeliest reason a solo user upgrades. Before buying Smartlead Base, confirm which reply features it includes.

Lead data and verification

Instantly's Lead Finder (formerly SuperSearch) Growth credits plan costs $47 a month for 1,500 credits or $77 for 2,000, and monthly-plan credits expire two months after purchase (both checked 5 November 2026). Smartlead's verified prospect email allowance is a $59/month add-on on Base and Pro and included on the Unlimited tiers. Either way, run lists through a verifier from our list cleaning tools guide before sending.

CRM handoff and client reporting

Neither built-in CRM replaces a full sales CRM; it triages interested replies, and webhooks push them onward to HubSpot, Pipedrive or a client's system. Smartlead sells its $29 workspaces as white-label. Instantly's pricing page doesn't describe white-label client access, so get written terms from sales if branded logins matter.

Where do Instantly and Smartlead fall short?

Instantly's split between Outreach and Credits makes bills harder to forecast, and its official pages disagreed on the Hypergrowth price and don't publish client workspace pricing. Smartlead's Base plan lacks CRM access, API and webhooks, and its useful extras are separate subscriptions.

  • Instantly: credits on monthly plans expire, and webhooks and full Unibox replies both wait for Hypergrowth.
  • Smartlead: workspace fees add up across many small clients, and servers and verification are billed separately.
  • Both: buying mailboxes and domains in-app ties your infrastructure to one vendor. Neither vendor's reply-rate or inbox placement claims were independently checked for this page.

What do the pricing pages leave out?

Pricing pages show caps and add-ons, but they don't show how long new mailboxes take to reach useful volume or how much admin each client adds. Those operating costs are where agency margins on cold email are usually won or lost, so treat the scenarios above as a floor.

Who carries the deliverability and policy risk?

You do, not the tool. Cold outreach must follow the rules where your recipients are, and mailbox providers enforce their own bulk-sender requirements on authentication, unsubscribe handling and complaint rates.

In the US that means CAN-SPAM; in the UK and EU, the GDPR and PECR also govern how you source data and contact people. Both tools provide unsubscribe handling and block lists, but lawful basis, accurate sender identity, honest subject lines and suppression across every mailbox remain your job. How hard is it to switch between Instantly and Smartlead?

Switching takes a billing cycle of overlap, mostly because mailboxes need to warm up again and webhooks need rebuilding. The one step you can't skip is moving your unsubscribe and block lists before any new sequence starts.

  1. Export campaigns, lead statuses, unsubscribes and block lists; import suppression data first.
  2. For vendor-bought mailboxes or domains, check whether they can move to your own Google or Microsoft tenant.
  3. Reconnect mailboxes and let warm-up run before restoring full volume.
  4. Rebuild webhooks and CRM mappings, then test that an interested reply creates the right record.
  5. Run both tools in parallel for one cycle so no live sequence is orphaned.

What else do buyers ask about Instantly vs Smartlead?

Most remaining questions are about per-mailbox charges, send limits and CRM. The short answers below rely on official pricing and help pages checked 4 and 5 November 2026.

Do Instantly or Smartlead charge per mailbox?

Neither prices its platform per connected mailbox; both advertise unlimited email accounts. You still pay a mailbox provider, or the vendor's own mailbox resale, for every inbox you send from.

Which is cheaper for an agency with many small clients?

Smartlead's $29 per-workspace fee is predictable but grows with every client you add. Instantly's subscription can cost less, but its client separation isn't publicly priced, so compare against a written quote.

What happens when I hit a send limit?

Instantly says you upgrade tiers rather than pay overage, or buy its $87/month Outreach add-on on Hypergrowth or Light Speed for 125,000 more emails and 25,000 more contacts. Smartlead's tiers also step up by send volume.

Does either tool include a CRM?

Instantly includes its CRM from Hypergrowth, and Smartlead provides CRM access from Pro. Most teams still pass qualified replies to a dedicated sales CRM.

Will either tool make cold email compliant?

No. They supply unsubscribe handling and block lists, but lawful basis, sender identity and honoring opt-outs stay with the sender.

What's the verdict on Instantly vs Smartlead?

Based on the product information reviewed, Smartlead is easier to budget for agencies that separate clients, and Instantly suits operators who want sending, data and AI agents under one roof on Hypergrowth or above. For a solo sender the subscriptions are close, and the mailboxes you buy will usually matter more.

Before starting either trial, price your own domains, mailboxes, verification and client count.
