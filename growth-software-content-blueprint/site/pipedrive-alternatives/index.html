Which Pipedrive alternative fits your reason for switching?

If marketing needs to share the CRM, look at HubSpot or ActiveCampaign. If reps live on the phone, look at Close; if reports and process rules are the problem, look at Zoho CRM or Salesforce; if the data model is the problem, look at Attio; and if price is the issue, start with Bigin.

  • Marketing and sales need one database: HubSpot Sales Hub, or ActiveCampaign if email nurture is the main growth lever.
  • Calling and sequences all day: Close.
  • Reports and process rules have outgrown the defaults: Zoho CRM, or Salesforce's small-business suites if you expect to grow into a platform.
  • Your relationships aren't just "deals": Attio.
  • You simply want to pay less: Bigin by Zoho CRM, or a free tier from our roundup of free CRM plans and their limits.

What does Pipedrive do well?

Pipedrive is built around a visual deal pipeline and the next activity on every deal, which keeps reps focused on what to do today. For a small team selling a straightforward product, that focus and its light setup are real strengths, not gaps.

Its core covers customizable pipelines and stages, email sync, activity scheduling, workflow automation and an integration marketplace. Plans are tiered and priced per seat, and some capabilities have been sold as add-ons. Check current tiers and add-ons on the official Pipedrive pricing page, since we didn't confirm them for this draft.

If those strengths still describe what your team needs, switching may cost more in disruption than it saves.

Why do small sales teams leave Pipedrive?

In our editorial assessment, the usual triggers are growing automation needs, harder reporting questions, marketing joining the CRM, a process that no longer fits one pipeline, and seat and add-on costs adding up. These are common decision patterns, not complaint data: we haven't surveyed Pipedrive customers.

  1. Automation: needs move beyond deal-stage triggers into nurture, lead scoring or multichannel sequences.
  2. Reporting: managers want forecasts by segment or source-to-revenue views.
  3. Marketing alignment: marketing wants campaigns, forms and lifecycle stages on the same records.
  4. Customization: partners, renewals or multi-product accounts don't fit a linear pipeline.
  5. Total cost: seats and add-ons accumulate faster than the team's revenue.

Which alternatives fix automation and marketing alignment?

HubSpot Sales Hub is the stronger pick when marketing generates most leads and wants campaigns and deals on one record. ActiveCampaign is the stronger pick when email nurture drives growth and the sales team is small.

HubSpot Sales Hub: best when marketing joins the CRM

Best for: teams where forms, campaigns and lifecycle stages need to sit on the same contact as the deal. Strengths: a shared database across HubSpot's hubs and a usable free tier. Limitations: sequences, forecasting and custom reporting tend to arrive together in a higher tier, and marketing automation needs Marketing Hub, priced separately. Migration note: decide early whether Pipedrive organizations become HubSpot companies with associations intact.

ActiveCampaign: best when email nurture is the growth lever

Best for: small businesses with a modest sales team acting on email engagement. Strengths: a visual automation builder, with CRM and deal features so sales can act on engagement data. Our ActiveCampaign and HubSpot platform comparison covers the trade-off in depth. Limitations: the pipeline is less central than in Pipedrive, and pricing scales with contacts as well as plan. Skip it if your reps, not your emails, close most deals. Migration note: move consent status and list membership as well as contacts and deals, and audit those fields before import.

Which alternative suits call-heavy outbound teams?

Close is the closest fit for inside-sales teams that make a lot of calls and run email sequences from the CRM. It puts calling, SMS, email and sequences in one workspace, so a small team may not need a separate engagement tool.

Limitations: it's a weaker fit for marketing-led teams, and calling features and costs vary by plan and country, so check the Close pricing page linked in the table. Migration note: Close organizes around leads (companies) with contacts underneath, which maps fairly cleanly to Pipedrive's organizations and persons. If you're weighing a dedicated engagement tool instead, see our sales engagement platforms for SDR teams.

Which alternatives give you deeper reporting and customization?

Zoho CRM gives cost-conscious teams custom modules, process rules and stronger reporting without an enterprise platform. Salesforce's small-business suites suit teams that expect complex processes later, and Attio suits teams whose relationships don't fit a single pipeline.

Zoho CRM: best for process rules on a budget

Strengths: broad customization, process-management features and a wider Zoho suite for analytics, help desk and finance. Limitations: the breadth makes setup heavier than Pipedrive, and features vary a lot by edition. Migration note: test custom-field mapping on a sample before the full import.

Salesforce small-business suites: best if you'll grow into a platform

Strengths: a packaged entry to Sales Cloud with a route to full editions later. Limitations: the suites carry automation and API caps, and anything beyond them assumes admin capacity. Our HubSpot versus Salesforce analysis models that ownership cost. Migration note: expect more mapping decisions, such as leads versus contacts and record types, than with any other option here.

Attio: best for relationship-shaped data

Strengths: a flexible data model with custom objects and list views, plus records enriched from connected email and calendars, based on the vendor's product information. Limitations: you design the structure yourself, and the ecosystem is younger than the incumbents'. Migration note: plan your objects before importing, because copying Pipedrive's structure wastes the reason for switching.

Which alternatives lower your total cost?

Bigin by Zoho CRM is the most direct lower-cost swap, because it's a pipeline-first CRM for very small teams with a simpler interface than Zoho CRM. Free CRM tiers are the other route, as long as their limits don't block your process.

Limitations: Bigin's automation and reporting are deliberately lighter, so a team with growing process needs may move again within a year or two. Cheaper licences don't always mean a lower total cost. Before switching purely on price, model seats, add-ons and admin time with our breakdown of CRM seat and add-on costs.

Does switching CRMs actually fix the problem?

Based on the product information reviewed, a switch fixes a capability gap, such as missing calling or marketing automation, more reliably than an adoption problem. If reps don't update Pipedrive today, a more powerful CRM is unlikely to change that on its own.

Who should stay on Pipedrive?

Stay if your team has fewer than about ten reps and sells one product through one or two pipelines. Also stay if your complaint is a single missing feature that an integration or add-on can supply.

A marketplace app or an automation platform often closes the gap with less disruption than a migration. Stay, too, if adoption is high: a CRM reps actually update is worth more than a more powerful one they avoid.

What should you check before you switch?

Check that you can export everything you'll need, that every automation and integration has a replacement plan, and that email history will link correctly in the new system. Then run a small test import before you set a cut-over date.

  1. Write down the switch reason and the measurable outcome you expect.
  2. Export deals, persons, organizations, activities, notes, products and custom fields, and archive the files.
  3. List every automation, integration and webhook, and decide which to rebuild.
  4. Confirm how historical emails will be linked to records after the move.
  5. Map stages, owners and custom fields, and delete fields nobody uses.
  6. Import a few hundred records and have two reps work in the new system for a week.
  7. Check your Pipedrive renewal date, freeze changes, and keep read-only access until it ends.

Agencies have extra needs, such as client separation and retainers, which our agency CRM shortlist handles with different criteria.

What else do buyers ask about Pipedrive alternatives?

Most questions are about like-for-like replacements, cost and keeping history intact. Confirm plan details on each vendor's official pricing page before deciding.

What is the closest like-for-like alternative to Pipedrive?

In our assessment, Bigin by Zoho CRM and Close feel closest day to day. Bigin is simpler and cheaper in intent, while Close is built more around outbound calling and sequences.

Is HubSpot more expensive than Pipedrive?

It depends on the tier you need. HubSpot's free tools and entry tier are inexpensive, but the tier with sequences and custom reporting costs considerably more per seat, so compare both vendors' current pricing pages for your seat count.

Can I keep my email history when I switch?

Usually in part. Many CRMs re-sync recent email from the connected inbox, but logged emails and notes need exporting, so confirm the method with the new vendor before cut-over.

How long does a Pipedrive migration take?

For a small team with clean data, a few weeks including testing is a realistic plan. Custom fields, automations and integrations each add time.

What's the verdict?

Pipedrive remains a sound choice for focused pipeline management. Leave it when a specific need, such as marketing alignment, calling, reporting, data structure or cost, consistently blocks the team, and pick the alternative built for that need.
