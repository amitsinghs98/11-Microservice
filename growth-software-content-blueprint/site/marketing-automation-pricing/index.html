How is marketing automation priced?

Marketing automation is priced on one of four units: contacts stored, tasks or executions run, events or profiles tracked, or seats. The unit you will grow fastest in decides your bill far more than the headline "from" price.

That is why two tools that both say "from $20 a month" can cost wildly different amounts for the same team. Before you compare vendors, translate each one into your own workload: contacts in six months, emails per month, workflow steps per lead and the number of people who need to log in. What do the four pricing units mean for your bill?

Contact-based plans grow with your list, task-based plans grow with workflow activity, event-based plans grow with product usage, and seat-based plans grow with headcount. Each one punishes a different kind of team.

Contact-based

Most email-led automation platforms tier by contacts or subscribers. Costs step up in bands, so adding a few dozen contacts can move the whole month into the next band. Ask whether unsubscribed, bounced or inactive contacts count; HubSpot, for example, separates marketing contacts from non-marketing contacts, so check its Marketing Hub pricing page for how that affects your tier.

Task-, credit- or execution-based

Connector platforms meter activity, and each counts it differently. Zapier bills tasks, meaning successful actions; its pricing FAQ says triggers, filters, Paths and failed actions don't use tasks (checked 4 November 2026). Make bills credits, with most module actions using one and the trigger module counting too (checked 4 November 2026). n8n bills one execution per full workflow run however many steps it has (checked 4 November 2026). Our Zapier vs Make vs n8n comparison walks through how the same workflow scores on each.

Event- or profile-based

Platforms built for SaaS lifecycle messaging often bill on tracked profiles or events rather than marketing contacts. They suit in-app and behavioural messaging more than newsletters, and the unit tracks product growth rather than list growth, so model it with your product team's usage numbers.

Seat-based

Seat pricing hurts agencies and teams where many people need occasional access. The lemlist pricing page shows the pattern clearly: its Email plan includes unlimited users, while Multichannel is listed at $109 per user per month billed monthly, or $87 billed yearly (checked 4 November 2026). Make, by contrast, lists unlimited users on all plans (checked 4 November 2026).

What do the workflow platforms charge at each tier?

For the three connector platforms, entry pricing is public and low; the jump comes when you need more volume, more users or self-hosting. All figures below were listed on the vendors' pricing pages on 4 November 2026, in US dollars.

  • Zapier: Free at 100 tasks a month with two-step Zaps; Professional from $19.99 a month for one user; Team from $69 a month with 25 users, per the Zapier pricing page (checked 4 November 2026). Task tiers above the starting level are selected on the page, so price your volume there.
  • Make: Free at 1,000 credits a month; at the 10,000-credit tier, Core is $9, Pro $16 and Teams $29 a month, per the Make pricing page (checked 4 November 2026). The page offers a monthly or annual toggle, so confirm which billing basis the figure you budget against uses.
  • n8n: Starter is $20 a month billed annually for 2,500 executions, Pro $50 a month billed annually for 10,000, and Business $800 a month billed annually for 40,000, self-hosted only, per the n8n pricing page (checked 4 November 2026). A self-hosted Community Edition carries no licence fee.

For contact-based suites, we could not confirm current tier prices for this draft, so we give none. Price MailerLite, Brevo, ActiveCampaign and HubSpot on their own pages using your contact count, and record the date you checked.

Should you pay monthly or annually?

Pay annually only once your list size, workflow volume and headcount have been stable for a quarter. Before that, the discount is usually smaller than the cost of being locked into the wrong tier.

The savings are real but modest. lemlist's Multichannel plan drops from $109 to $87 per user per month on yearly billing (checked 4 November 2026), roughly a fifth off. Before committing, ask whether you can move up mid-term, whether moving down has to wait for renewal, and whether unused credits expire at the end of the term. Make's pricing page says credits expire at the end of the term (checked 4 November 2026), which matters if your usage is seasonal.

Which costs do budgets usually miss?

The lines most often missed are mandatory onboarding, extra seats, contact-band jumps, usage overages and premium support. Any one of them can exceed the subscription in year one for a small team.

  • Onboarding: some CRM-native suites require a one-time onboarding fee on their higher editions. Ask whether it is mandatory, and whether it can be waived or credited.
  • Overages: Zapier's pricing FAQ describes pay-per-task usage beyond your limit at a higher rate (checked 4 November 2026). Decide in advance whether you would rather pay or pause.
  • Contact bands: plan for the band you will reach in six months, not the one you are in today.
  • Premium support: phone support, named account managers and migration help are often tied to higher tiers or sold separately. n8n, for example, lists forum support below Enterprise (checked 4 November 2026).
  • Channel add-ons: SMS, WhatsApp, extra landing pages and dedicated sending IPs are usually priced apart from email.
  • The CRM half of the bill: if automation and CRM are separate tools, our breakdown of CRM seat, onboarding and admin costs covers the other side.

Price lists rarely show the cost that matters most for small teams: the hours someone spends building and fixing workflows.

What would three typical small teams pay?

For a solo operator connecting a form to a CRM and Slack, the connector layer costs roughly $108 to $240 a year at the prices we checked. For a five-person agency running about 2,000 automated actions a month, it starts at roughly $240 to $828 a year before any contact-based platform is added.

All three scenarios are hypothetical, use only prices listed on 4 November 2026, and exclude tax, SMS and staff time.

Scenario A: solo consultant, connector layer only

Assumptions: one user, 300 new leads a month, and a workflow that adds each lead to the CRM, tags it and posts a Slack alert (three actions after the trigger).

  • Zapier: 900 tasks a month and a multi-step Zap, so Free is out. Professional starts at $19.99 a month, at least $239.88 a year; confirm that the tier you select covers 900 tasks.
  • Make: about four credits per lead, or 1,200 a month, which exceeds Free. Core at the 10,000-credit tier is $9 a month, or $108 a year.
  • n8n Cloud: 300 executions a month fits Starter at $20 a month billed annually, or $240 a year.

Scenario B: three-person B2B marketing team

Assumptions: three users, 2,000 marketing contacts, multi-step nurture and lead scoring, with handoff to sales. This team needs a contact-based suite, and we could not confirm those prices for this draft, so use this worksheet instead of a total.

  • Annual subscription = the monthly price of the tier that covers 2,000 contacts and scoring × 12.
  • Plus any one-time onboarding fee in year one.
  • Plus seats beyond those included, × 12.
  • Plus the connector layer from Scenario A if the suite lacks a native integration you need.

Scenario C: small agency automation layer

Assumptions: five staff build and monitor client automations, about 2,000 actions a month across lead routing and reporting alerts, and everyone needs a login.

  • Zapier Team: from $69 a month with 25 users, so at least $828 a year; price the task tier for 2,000 tasks on the page.
  • Make Teams: $29 a month at 10,000 credits, or $348 a year, with unlimited users.
  • n8n: if each workflow run carries several actions, 2,000 actions may be well under 2,500 executions, so Starter at $240 a year could cover it; Pro, at $50 a month billed annually for 10,000 executions, is $600 a year with headroom.

The cheapest line is not automatically right: Zapier's linear builder is easiest for non-technical staff to maintain. The guide to workflow automation tools for agencies covers multi-client set-ups.

Can a free plan replace the first paid tier?

Only for a single, simple workflow. Free plans are permanent but capped, trials are temporary, and production automations should never run on a trial.

Zapier Free allows 100 tasks a month and Make Free 1,000 credits a month (checked 4 November 2026), which can carry a low-volume handoff for a while. Our guide to free marketing automation tools and their upgrade triggers models when each runs out.

What should you ask sales before you sign?

Ask which contacts count toward billing, what happens at the limit, and what the price will be at renewal. Get the answers in writing, because those three questions cover most surprise invoices.

  • Which contacts count: all stored, marketing only, or active only?
  • What happens if we exceed the contact, send or task limit mid-term: overage, pause or forced upgrade?
  • Is onboarding mandatory at this tier, and can it be waived or credited?
  • Is the quoted price introductory, and what is the renewal price?
  • Which support channels are included, and what costs extra?
  • Can we move down a tier at renewal without losing data or workflows?
  • Are SMS, extra seats, sandboxes or API access billed separately?

The same questions apply to email-first platforms; our email marketing software shortlist shows how packaging differs there.

What should you check before each renewal?

Check usage against the next band, the renewal date and notice period, and whether another tool in your stack now duplicates the feature you are paying for. Do it a month before renewal, not the week of.

  • Contacts within 10% of the next band, or task usage above 80% of the allowance.
  • A feature you need, such as lead scoring or custom reporting, that sits one tier up.
  • Seat requests from sales or client-services staff.
  • Expiry of any introductory discount.

Prune unengaged contacts and archive dead workflows before the renewal quote is calculated. What else do buyers ask about marketing automation pricing?

These are the questions that come up once the quotes arrive. Each answer stands on its own.

Why can't I compare automation tools on price per contact alone?

Because many charge by sends, tasks, executions or seats instead. Convert each tool to your actual workload first: contacts in six months, emails per month, workflow steps per lead and number of users.

Is a task the same thing on Zapier, Make and n8n?

No. Zapier counts successful actions as tasks, Make counts module operations as credits, and n8n counts one execution per full workflow run regardless of how many steps it has.

Does annual billing always save money?

Only if your tier fits for the whole year. A discount of around a fifth is easily wiped out by being locked into a tier that is too large, or by credits that expire unused.

Which marketing automation prices are not public?

Enterprise tiers, onboarding packages and premium support are often quote-only. Ask for them in writing, with the renewal price, before you compare quotes.
