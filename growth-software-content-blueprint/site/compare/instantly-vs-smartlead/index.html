Quick verdict

  • Solo founder or consultant sending a few thousand emails a month: Smartlead Base is slightly cheaper and allows more stored contacts, but it has no CRM access and no API. Instantly Growth costs a little more and also lacks webhooks and full in-app replies. Choose based on whether you'll reply from inside the tool.
  • Agency running separate client programmes: Smartlead publishes a per-client workspace price and has a top tier with workspaces included, so you can forecast the cost per client. Instantly's pricing page doesn't price client separation, so ask before you commit.
  • Team that also needs lead data and AI agents: Instantly sells a separate credits subscription, plus bundles that pair outreach with credits. Smartlead includes a verified prospect email allowance that is free only on its two Unlimited tiers.
  • High-volume sender who wants managed infrastructure: both offer dedicated server or IP options at the upper tiers (Instantly's SISR on Light Speed, Smartlead's SmartServers). Neither removes your obligations on consent, opt-outs and list quality.

In our editorial assessment, neither tool is the cheaper one in every case. The answer changes with how many clients you separate, how many mailboxes you buy and whether you need webhooks at the entry tier.

How the two products are positioned

Instantly now sells itself as a modular outreach system. Its pricing page splits Outreach plans (sending, warm-up, the Unibox inbox) from Instantly Credits, which pay for its B2B lead database lookups, enrichment and AI agents. Bundles combine the two. The built-in CRM comes with the Hypergrowth tier and above rather than as a separate product.

Smartlead prices on send volume, stored contacts and a verified prospect email allowance. It sells add-ons for client workspaces with white-labelling, dedicated servers, inbox placement testing, verification credits and a dialer. Its pricing documentation describes it as charging neither per seat nor per mailbox, and its pitch to agencies rests mostly on the workspace model.

Both belong to the cold email category we cover in our shortlist of cold email software for B2B agencies. If your reps also work calls, LinkedIn tasks and CRM-driven cadences, look at sales engagement platforms built for SDR teams instead.

Workflow-by-workflow analysis

Mailbox and domain setup

"Unlimited email accounts" doesn't mean free sending capacity. Each mailbox comes from Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or an SMTP provider, and each needs a domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up. Both vendors now resell mailboxes and domains in-app, which saves setup time. It also concentrates your infrastructure with one vendor, which matters if you leave later. Pre-warmed mailboxes cost roughly double at both vendors, and they don't replace a sensible ramp-up.

Warm-up and deliverability controls

Both include automated warm-up on every plan. Smartlead tiers the quality of its warm-up pool, with Premium and Ultrapremium only on the Unlimited plans. Instantly keeps its private server and IP rotation (SISR) for Light Speed. Smartlead sells dedicated SmartServers at $39 per server per month from Pro and includes three on Unlimited Prime. For inbox placement testing, Smartlead sells SmartDelivery as a separate subscription starting at $49/month, checked 4 November 2026. Many teams pair either tool with an independent monitor from our review of email deliverability tools.

Inbox management and replies

Instantly's Unibox only previews replies on Growth. Full in-app replying, custom reply labels and blocklist triggers start on Hypergrowth. That is the main reason a solo user outgrows the entry tier. Smartlead offers a master inbox across connected mailboxes. Check whether the reply-management features you need sit on Base or need Pro.

CRM handoff and integrations

Neither built-in CRM replaces a full sales CRM for most agencies. In practice, the CRM features help triage interested replies before you push them to HubSpot, Pipedrive or a client's system. Webhooks and API matter more for that handoff, and they're gated: Instantly offers webhooks from Hypergrowth, and Smartlead offers API and webhooks from Pro. If you run handoffs through Zapier or Make, budget for the higher tier.

Lead data and verification

Instantly's credits pay for lookups in its lead database, enrichment and AI agents. The Growth credits plan was listed at $47 to $77/month for 1,500 to 2,000 credits, and credits on monthly plans expire two months after purchase (checked 4 November 2026). Smartlead bundles a verified prospect email allowance, which is a $59/month add-on on Base and Pro and included on the Unlimited tiers. It sells separate verification credits starting at $15/month for 6,000. Whichever data source you use, verify lists before sending. Our comparison of list cleaning tools covers independent verifiers.

Reporting for clients

Smartlead lets agencies white-label client workspaces for $29/month per workspace. Instantly's pricing page didn't describe white-label client reporting, so if branded client logins matter to you, ask for a demo of how Instantly separates clients.

Limitations of both

  • Instantly: the split between Outreach and Credits makes the bill harder to forecast, monthly-plan credits expire after two months, webhooks and full Unibox replies need Hypergrowth, and client workspace pricing isn't published.
  • Smartlead: Base has no CRM access, API or webhooks. Per-workspace fees add up across many small clients. Inbox placement testing, dedicated servers and verification are all separate purchases.
  • Both: buying mailboxes in-app ties your infrastructure to the vendor. "Unlimited" mailbox claims sit under fair-use terms. Neither vendor's figures for reply rates or inbox placement were independently checked here.

The responsibility rests with you, not the tool. Cold outreach must follow the rules where your recipients are, including CAN-SPAM in the US and the GDPR and PECR in the UK and EU. Mailbox providers also enforce their own bulk-sender requirements on authentication, one-click unsubscribe and complaint rates. Scenario fit

Choose Instantly if you want one vendor for sending, lead data and AI reply or prospecting agents, you'll run on Hypergrowth or above anyway, and you don't need branded client logins.

Choose Smartlead if you're an agency that needs to separate and white-label clients at a known price per client, you want to store many contacts on the Unlimited tiers, or you want dedicated servers without jumping to the top plan.

Consider neither if your team needs calls, LinkedIn tasks and rep-level cadence reporting. A multichannel tool fits better, and our lemlist and Reply.io comparison shows the per-seat trade-off. If you're building the prospect list first, start with B2B contact databases and sales intelligence tools.

Switching considerations

  1. Export campaigns, sequences, lead statuses, unsubscribes and the global block list before you cancel. Moving the suppression list is the step you can't skip.
  2. If you bought mailboxes or domains through the vendor, check the transfer terms for each domain and whether the mailboxes can move to your own Google or Microsoft tenant.
  3. Reconnect mailboxes to the new tool and let warm-up run before you restart full volume.
  4. Rebuild webhooks and CRM mappings, then test that an interested reply creates the right record downstream.
  5. Run both tools in parallel for one billing cycle so no live sequence is left without an owner.

FAQs

Do Instantly or Smartlead charge per mailbox?

Neither charges a platform fee per connected mailbox. Both list unlimited email accounts on every plan. You still pay a mailbox provider or the vendor's own mailbox resale for each inbox.

Which is cheaper for an agency?

It depends on the number of clients. Smartlead's $29 per-workspace fee is predictable but grows with your client roster. Instantly's base subscription can cost less, but its client separation isn't priced publicly, so get a written quote before comparing.

Does either include a CRM?

Instantly includes its CRM from Hypergrowth, and Smartlead provides CRM access from Pro, based on pricing pages checked 4 November 2026. Most teams still pass qualified replies to a dedicated CRM.

What happens when I hit a send limit?

Instantly says you upgrade tiers rather than pay overages, or buy its $87/month Outreach add-on on eligible plans. Smartlead's tiers also step up by send volume.

Do these tools make cold email compliant?

No. They provide unsubscribe handling and block lists, but lawful basis, accurate sender identity, honest subject lines and honouring opt-outs remain your responsibility.

Verdict

Based on the product information reviewed, Smartlead is easier to budget for an agency that separates clients, because the workspace cost is published and the Unlimited tiers remove the contact cap. Instantly suits operators who want sending, lead data and AI agents from one vendor and will run on Hypergrowth or above. For a solo sender the subscriptions are close, and the mailboxes you buy will usually matter more.
