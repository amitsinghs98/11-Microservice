Should you choose Instantly or Smartlead?

Choose Smartlead if you're an agency that needs to separate clients at a published per-client price. Choose Instantly if you want sending, lead data and AI agents from one vendor and you'll run on its Hypergrowth tier or above anyway.

  • Solo founder or consultant sending a few thousand emails a month: the entry plans are close in price. Smartlead Base is cheaper and stores more contacts, but it has no CRM access, API or webhooks. Instantly Growth only previews replies in its inbox. Decide based on whether you'll reply from inside the tool.
  • Agency running separate client programs: Smartlead prices client workspaces individually and includes three on its top tier, so you can forecast cost per client. Instantly's pricing page doesn't price client separation, so get a written quote.
  • Team that also buys lead data: Instantly sells a separate credits subscription for its lead database, enrichment and AI agents. Smartlead bundles a verified prospect email allowance that is an add-on on its lower tiers.
  • High-volume sender wanting managed infrastructure: both sell dedicated server options at upper tiers. Neither changes your obligations on consent, opt-outs and list quality.

In our editorial assessment, neither tool is cheaper in every case. The answer moves with your client count, your mailbox count and whether you need webhooks at the entry tier.

How are Instantly and Smartlead positioned?

Instantly sells a modular system: Outreach plans for sending and warm-up, plus a separate Credits product for lead data and AI agents. Smartlead prices on send volume and stored contacts, and builds its agency pitch around paid client workspaces and infrastructure add-ons.

On Instantly's pricing page, Outreach plans cover sending, warm-up and the Unibox reply inbox, while Instantly Credits pay for database lookups, enrichment and AI agents (checked 4 November 2026). Its built-in CRM comes with the Hypergrowth tier and above rather than as a separate purchase.

Smartlead's pricing page sells add-ons for white-labelled client workspaces, dedicated SmartServers, inbox placement testing and verification credits (checked 4 November 2026). Both tools sit in the sending-first group of our cold email software shortlist for B2B agencies. If your reps also work calls and LinkedIn tasks, compare sales engagement platforms for SDR teams instead.

What do Instantly and Smartlead really cost for a solo sender and an agency?

For one person sending about 4,000 emails a month, the modeled totals are roughly $81 on Instantly and $69 on Smartlead. For a five-client agency, Smartlead's published workspace fees push it to about $531 a month, while Instantly's comparable figure is about $432 plus whatever client separation costs once quoted.

Both scenarios are hypothetical. They use list prices checked 4 November 2026, monthly billing, US dollars and no promotions (Smartlead was advertising 50% off the first month, which we ignore). We assume about 30 cold emails per mailbox per working day over 22 working days. That's a conservative planning assumption, not a vendor rule.

Scenario A: what does one-person outbound cost?

Assumptions: 3 domains, 6 fresh Google mailboxes, about 4,000 sends and fewer than 1,000 new contacts a month, with lead data sourced elsewhere.

  • Instantly: Growth $47 + six done-for-you mailboxes at $5 ($30) + three domains at $15/year (about $3.75 a month) = about $81 a month.
  • Smartlead: Base $39 + six fresh mailboxes at $4.50 ($27) + three domains at $13/year (about $3.25 a month) = about $69 a month.

The $12 gap matters less than the feature gap. If replying in-app or pushing replies to a CRM matters, the honest comparison is Instantly Hypergrowth at $107 against Smartlead Pro at $94.

Scenario B: what does a five-client agency pay?

Assumptions: 5 client programs kept separate, 20 domains, 60 mailboxes, about 40,000 sends and 15,000 new contacts a month, with webhooks needed for CRM handoff.

  • Instantly: Hypergrowth $107 + 60 mailboxes ($300) + 20 domains (about $25 a month) = about $432 a month, plus client separation, which isn't publicly priced.
  • Smartlead: Pro $94 + five workspaces at $29 ($145) + 60 mailboxes ($270) + 20 domains (about $22 a month) = about $531 a month.

Mailboxes cost more than either platform in this scenario, which is the pattern to expect at agency scale. Verification comes on top: Smartlead lists verification credits from $15/month for 6,000 and $59/month for 24,000 (checked 4 November 2026). How do warm-up, replies, data and CRM handoff differ day to day?

The biggest daily difference is reply handling: Instantly keeps full in-app replies and webhooks for Hypergrowth, while Smartlead keeps CRM access and webhooks for Pro. Warm-up and dedicated infrastructure exist on both, but the better options sit higher up each price list.

Warm-up and infrastructure

Both include automated warm-up, and Smartlead tiers the quality of its warm-up pool by plan. Instantly reserves its private server and IP rotation for Light Speed. Smartlead sells SmartServers at $39 per server per month from Pro, and its SmartDelivery inbox placement testing starts at $49/month (checked 4 November 2026). For an independent check, many teams pair either tool with a monitor from our email deliverability tools comparison.

Inbox management

Instantly's pricing page shows the Unibox as preview-only on Growth, with full replying from Hypergrowth (checked 4 November 2026). That gate is the most likely reason a solo user moves up a tier. Before buying Smartlead Base, confirm which reply-management features it includes.

Lead data and verification

Instantly's Growth credits plan was listed at $47 to $77 a month for 1,500 to 2,000 credits, and credits on monthly plans expire two months after purchase (checked 4 November 2026). Smartlead's verified prospect email allowance is a $59/month add-on on Base and Pro and included on the Unlimited tiers. Whatever the data source, run lists through a verifier from our list cleaning tools guide before sending.

CRM handoff and client reporting

Neither built-in CRM replaces a full sales CRM for most agencies. It triages interested replies, and webhooks then push them to HubSpot, Pipedrive or a client's system. Smartlead's $29 workspaces support white-labeling for client logins. Instantly's pricing page doesn't describe white-label client access, so ask for a demo if branded logins matter.

Where do Instantly and Smartlead fall short?

Instantly's split between Outreach and Credits makes bills harder to forecast, and it doesn't publish client workspace pricing. Smartlead's Base plan lacks CRM access, API and webhooks, and its useful extras are all separate subscriptions.

  • Instantly: monthly-plan credits expire after two months, webhooks and full Unibox replies need Hypergrowth, and agencies can't price client separation without talking to sales.
  • Smartlead: workspace fees add up across many small clients, and inbox placement testing, dedicated servers and verification are each billed separately.
  • Both: buying mailboxes and domains in-app concentrates your infrastructure with one vendor, which complicates leaving. Neither vendor's reply-rate or inbox placement claims were independently checked for this page.

What do the pricing pages leave out?

Pricing pages show caps and add-ons, but they don't show how long new mailboxes take to reach useful volume or how much admin each client adds. Those operating costs are where agency margins on cold email are usually won or lost, so treat the scenarios above as a floor.

Who carries the deliverability and policy risk?

You do, not the tool. Cold outreach must follow the rules where your recipients are, and mailbox providers enforce their own bulk-sender requirements on authentication, unsubscribe handling and complaint rates.

In the US that means CAN-SPAM; in the UK and EU, the GDPR and PECR also apply to how you source data and contact people. Both tools provide unsubscribe handling and block lists. Lawful basis, accurate sender identity, honest subject lines and suppression across every mailbox remain your job. How hard is it to switch between Instantly and Smartlead?

Switching takes a billing cycle of overlap, mostly because mailboxes need to warm up again and webhooks need rebuilding. The one step you can't skip is moving your unsubscribe and block lists before any new sequence starts.

  1. Export campaigns, lead statuses, unsubscribes and the global block list, and import the suppression data first.
  2. If you bought mailboxes or domains through the vendor, check whether domains transfer and whether mailboxes can move to your own Google or Microsoft tenant.
  3. Reconnect mailboxes and let warm-up run before restoring full volume.
  4. Rebuild webhooks and CRM mappings, then confirm an interested reply creates the right downstream record.
  5. Run both tools in parallel for one cycle so no live sequence is orphaned.

What else do buyers ask about Instantly vs Smartlead?

Most remaining questions are about per-mailbox charges, send limits and CRM. The short answers below rely on pricing pages checked 4 November 2026.

Do Instantly or Smartlead charge per mailbox?

Neither prices its platform per connected mailbox. You still pay a mailbox provider, or the vendor's own mailbox resale, for every inbox you send from.

Which is cheaper for an agency with many small clients?

Smartlead's $29 per-workspace fee is predictable but grows with every client you add. Instantly's subscription can cost less, but its client separation isn't publicly priced, so compare against a written quote.

What happens when I hit a send limit?

Instantly says you upgrade tiers rather than pay overage, or buy its $87/month Outreach add-on on eligible plans for 125,000 more emails and 25,000 more contacts. Smartlead's tiers also step up by send volume.

Does either tool include a CRM?

Instantly includes its CRM from Hypergrowth, and Smartlead provides CRM access from Pro. Most teams still pass qualified replies to a dedicated sales CRM.

Will either tool make cold email compliant?

No. They supply unsubscribe handling and block lists, but lawful basis, sender identity and honoring opt-outs stay with the sender.

What's the verdict on Instantly vs Smartlead?

Based on the product information reviewed, Smartlead is easier to budget for agencies that separate clients, and Instantly suits operators who want sending, data and AI agents under one roof on Hypergrowth or above. For a solo sender the subscriptions are close, and the mailboxes you buy will usually matter more.

Before you start a trial on either vendor's site, price your own domains, mailboxes, verification and client count.
