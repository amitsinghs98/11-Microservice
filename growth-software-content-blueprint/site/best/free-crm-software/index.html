Best Free CRM Software for Startups: Limits Before You Commit

Most free CRM plans are generous for one founder and tight for a team of three. This guide maps user caps, record limits and upgrade triggers so you know which wall you will hit first.

Quick answer

The user cap matters more than the feature list. On the official pricing pages we reviewed on 4 November 2026, HubSpot, Capsule and Bitrix24 cap their free plans at two users, while Zoho CRM and Attio allow three. That single number decides most startup shortlists.

  • Solo founder planning to add marketing: HubSpot's free tools, where email, forms, meetings and reporting share one database.
  • Three-person team wanting automation now: Zoho CRM Free, whose pricing page lists workflow automation on the free edition.
  • Relationship-heavy founding team: Attio Free, with a flexible data model and enrichment, but only three reports.
  • Very small client list: Capsule Free, if you stay under 250 contacts.
  • CRM plus tasks and chat: Bitrix24 Free, a basic CRM inside a wider workspace.
  • Engineering time but no budget: self-hosted open source such as Twenty or SuiteCRM.

Who this guide is for

Pre-seed and seed founders, and small service firms, who have outgrown a spreadsheet but cannot yet justify per-seat software. The free CRM options

1. HubSpot free tools

Best for: one or two founders who want CRM, basic email marketing and meeting links together. The HubSpot pricing page lists the free tier as free for up to 2 users with no credit card required (checked 4 November 2026).

Limits: the same page lists one personal meetings link, up to 3 email templates, 2,000 marketing email sends per calendar month with HubSpot branding, and 10 dashboards of 50 reports each. Sales automation workflows and up to 15 pipelines are listed against Starter, not the free column.

Strengths and limitations: a broad free toolset and a large integration ecosystem, but the two-user cap arrives quickly and higher tiers are where costs climb; our HubSpot and Salesforce comparison covers that path. HubSpot was advertising promotional Starter pricing for new customers when we checked, so ask about renewal price. Skip it if your founding team is already three people.

2. Zoho CRM Free

Best for: up to three users who want simple automation without paying. The Zoho CRM pricing page describes the free edition as forever free for 3 users, with workflow automation, up to 10 custom email templates, standard reports, mobile apps and 5,000 API calls per day (checked 4 November 2026).

Strengths and limitations: the most permissive user cap among the hosted products here, with automation included. But the same page says data export is free for up to 10 times, and multiple pipelines, email integration and calendar integration are listed under the paid Standard edition. Skip it if your team strongly prefers a minimal interface over configuration depth.

3. Attio Free

Best for: founder-led sales and fundraising teams tracking relationships across people, companies and lists. The Attio pricing page lists up to 3 seats, up to 3 objects, 50,000 records, automatic data enrichment, 200 emails per month and up to 3 reports on the free plan (checked 4 November 2026).

Strengths and limitations: a flexible data model and company enrichment fields such as employee count and funding raised. Attio also advertises up to 80% off for eligible startups. Against that, three reports is very little once investors want pipeline views, the free plan lists no access permissions, and paid plans add AI and workflow credits that make costs harder to forecast. Skip it if you need role-based visibility from day one.

4. Capsule Free

Best for: consultancies with a short client list. The Capsule pricing page describes a free plan for up to 2 users with 250 contacts, 5 custom fields and 1 sales pipeline, and states there is no setup or onboarding fee on any plan (checked 4 November 2026).

Strengths and limitations: a clean contact-and-pipeline tool, but 250 contacts is the tightest record limit here, and workflow automations are listed from the Growth plan. Every prospect, referrer and investor uses a contact. Skip it if you plan to import a prospect list.

5. Bitrix24 Free

Best for: tiny teams wanting a CRM, task boards, chat and video calls in one workspace. The Bitrix24 pricing page lists the free plan for 1 to 2 users with 5 GB storage and basic CRM covering deals and contacts (checked 4 November 2026).

Strengths and limitations: paid plans use a flat rate per organization that includes a set number of users, which can beat per-seat pricing as you grow. But sales pipelines and email integration are listed from the paid Basic plan, so the free CRM is genuinely basic, and the wider workspace can feel heavy. Skip it if your team already lives in Slack and Google Workspace.

6. Twenty (open source, self-hosted)

Best for: technical founders comfortable running their own application. Twenty is an open-source CRM you can host yourself; based on the product information reviewed, it also sells a hosted version. You avoid licence fees and control your data, but you pay in hosting, patching and backups, and the integration catalogue is smaller than the large vendors'. Skip it if nobody wants on-call responsibility for a database.

7. SuiteCRM (open source, self-hosted)

Best for: teams that want a traditional, highly configurable CRM and have admin skills or a partner. SuiteCRM is a long-established open-source CRM with sales, marketing and service modules, and its vendor sells hosting and support separately. Setup is heavier than the hosted options above. Skip it if you want to be productive in an afternoon.

Solo founder and 3-person team scenarios

Scenario A: solo founder, founder-led sales

Hypothetical assumptions: one user, about 400 contacts, one pipeline and a website demo form. Every hosted option fits on user count; Capsule fails on contacts. HubSpot's free tools are the most complete fit because forms, a meetings link and email live together, though the free meetings link and emails carry HubSpot branding.

Scenario B: three-person founding team

Hypothetical assumptions: two founders and a first seller, about 3,000 contacts, a sales pipeline and a partnerships pipeline. The two-user caps rule out HubSpot, Capsule and Bitrix24 unless someone stays off the CRM. That leaves Zoho CRM Free if automation matters most, or Attio Free if relationship context matters most. On Zoho, the second pipeline is the likely paying trigger, since multiple pipelines are listed under Standard.

When that upgrade comes, model the full cost rather than the headline seat price. Our CRM pricing breakdown works through 5-seat and 15-seat totals, including onboarding and admin time.

Free plan or free trial

A free plan has no end date but has caps; a trial unlocks a paid tier briefly and then stops. Attio offers a 14-day Pro trial and Capsule a 14-day trial of paid plans with no card required (checked 4 November 2026). Capsule's page notes you must add a payment card when a trial ends to keep that account's data.

Based on our editorial knowledge, Pipedrive and Salesforce's entry editions are sold as paid plans with trials rather than permanent free tiers. Confirm on the Pipedrive pricing page and Salesforce sales pricing page. If Pipedrive appeals, our list of Pipedrive alternatives for small sales teams covers similar pipeline-first tools.

Data export and migration risk

Startups put their earliest, messiest data into free plans, and leaving is harder than joining. Check three things before importing.

  • Export limits. Zoho's free edition lists exports as free for up to 10 times. Check what every vendor exports for activities, notes and email history, not just contacts.
  • Lost history. Contacts and deals usually export cleanly; email threads and stage-change history often do not.
  • Custom objects. A data model built on Attio's custom objects may not map neatly onto a conventional CRM later.

Keep a field dictionary from day one: name, purpose, allowed values and owner. It makes a later move to a CRM built for B2B SaaS sales teams far less painful.

Buying mistakes and questions to ask

  • Ignoring the user cap that will force an upgrade at your next hire.
  • Importing a cold prospect list into a contact-capped plan.
  • Assuming promotional pricing is the renewal price.
  • Letting each founder create fields before agreeing pipeline stages.
  • Paying for a separate email tool when the CRM's allowance would do; compare what free email marketing plans actually allow first.

Ask each vendor: what triggers the first paid tier, whether activities and email history export, what happens to data on downgrade, and whether a first-year discount is also the renewal price.

FAQs

Which free CRM allows the most users?

Among the hosted plans we checked on 4 November 2026, Zoho CRM Free (3 users) and Attio Free (up to 3 seats). Self-hosted open-source tools have no vendor seat licence, but you take on hosting and admin.

Is HubSpot's CRM really free?

HubSpot lists a free tier for up to 2 users with no credit card required. It has feature limits and HubSpot branding, and sales automation workflows are listed from Starter.

Can a free CRM handle outbound prospecting?

Rarely well. Contact caps, email limits and missing sequences push outbound teams to paid tiers. If nurture is part of the plan, see our guide to free marketing automation tools and their upgrade triggers.

When should a startup start paying for a CRM?

Usually when a hire exceeds the user cap, you need a second pipeline, or you need permissions for a new seller. Those are operational triggers, not feature wishes.

Verdict

Our editorial assessment: for a solo founder, HubSpot's free tools are the most connected starting point. For a three-person team, Zoho CRM Free and Attio Free are the realistic choices because they seat everyone. Capsule and Bitrix24 suit narrower cases, and self-hosted tools suit teams who would rather spend engineering time than money.
