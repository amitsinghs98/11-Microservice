Which product recommendation engine should you choose?

Shopify stores should start with Shopify's free Search & Discovery recommendations and move to Rebuy, LimeSpot or Nosto when they need more placements and controls. Teams with developers and custom storefronts should compare Algolia Recommend, Recombee, Amazon Personalize and Google Cloud's Vertex AI Search for commerce.

  • Free starting point on Shopify: Shopify Search & Discovery.
  • Cart, checkout-adjacent and post-purchase recommendations on Shopify: Rebuy.
  • Merchandiser-friendly recommendation widgets for Shopify and similar platforms: LimeSpot.
  • Recommendations as part of a broader personalization layer: Nosto.
  • Teams already using Algolia for search: Algolia Recommend.
  • Developer teams wanting a dedicated recommendation API: Recombee.
  • AWS-based engineering teams building their own models: Amazon Personalize.
  • Google Cloud teams with large catalogs: Vertex AI Search for commerce.

Which recommendation placements matter most for an online store?

Related products on product pages and frequently-bought-together blocks near add-to-cart usually do the most work, because they meet shoppers at a decision point. Recently viewed and personalized ranking matter more as returning traffic grows.

  • Related products: similar items by attribute or co-viewing, useful when the first product isn't quite right.
  • Frequently bought together: complements based on co-purchase, which lift order value when the pairing is genuine.
  • Recently viewed: a simple memory aid that needs no model, only reliable session and profile tracking.
  • Personalized ranking: re-ordering category, search or homepage products for each shopper. This blurs into personalization, covered in our guide to ecommerce personalization software.

Fewer, better placements beat a widget on every template. Each block competes for attention with the main product and adds page weight.

What is each engine good at, and when should you skip it?

Each block names the buyer, the main strength and the main reason to look elsewhere. Confirm placement limits, order or request caps and contract terms on the official pages.

Shopify Search & Discovery: best free baseline

Shopify's Search & Discovery app lets merchants manage product recommendations alongside filters and search settings, including adding complementary products manually. For a small catalog, hand-picked complements often beat an algorithm with little data. Skip it once you need personalized ranking, many placements or testing.

Rebuy: best for cart and post-purchase recommendations

Rebuy concentrates on recommendations and offers around the cart and after purchase on Shopify, combining rules with automated suggestions. That suits brands with obvious add-ons or replenishment products. Consider another option if your main gap is product-page discovery rather than order value.

LimeSpot: best for merchandiser-run widgets

LimeSpot provides personalized recommendation widgets and merchandising controls aimed at ecommerce teams rather than developers. It suits stores that want several placements managed from one dashboard. Check its testing and reporting depth against your needs before committing.

Nosto: best when recommendations are one part of personalization

Nosto includes recommendations within a broader commerce experience platform covering content, category merchandising and search. Buy it if you'll use that breadth. If recommendations are your only need, a narrower tool will be simpler.

Algolia Recommend: best for stores already on Algolia

Algolia Recommend adds related-product and frequently-bought-together models to Algolia's search platform, reusing the same index and event data. That reuse is the main reason to choose it. Without Algolia search already in place, the integration work is harder to justify.

Recombee: best dedicated recommendation API

Recombee is a recommendation-as-a-service API that developers feed with catalog items and interactions, then query for recommendations. It suits custom storefronts and headless builds where you want to control the front end. It needs engineering for integration and ongoing data quality.

Amazon Personalize: best for AWS teams building their own recommender

Amazon Personalize is an AWS machine learning service for building recommendation systems from your interaction, item and user data. It gives data teams model flexibility inside their existing AWS stack. There's no merchandiser-facing widget layer, so skip it unless engineering will own it long term.

Vertex AI Search for commerce: best for Google Cloud teams with large catalogs

Vertex AI Search for commerce is Google Cloud's retail search and recommendations offering. It suits large retailers already running data pipelines on Google Cloud. The setup effort around catalog and event ingestion is significant, so it's rarely a fit for a small team.

How do recommendation engines handle cold starts and merchandising control?

Cold starts (new shoppers, new products, new stores) are handled with fallbacks such as bestsellers, attribute similarity or manual picks until behavior data builds up. Merchandising controls let you override the model, and you need them for stock, margin and brand rules.

  • Catalog sync: ask how often product, price and inventory changes reach the engine, so it doesn't recommend sold-out items.
  • Controls: check for pin, boost, bury and exclude rules, and whether they apply per placement or globally.
  • Latency: server-side or edge delivery avoids widgets that load after the shopper has scrolled past.
  • Reports: look for placement-level views of clicks, add-to-carts and orders, plus a way to export raw events.

How do you test whether recommendations add incremental revenue?

Run a holdout: show the recommendation placement to one randomly assigned group and a control (no widget or a simple bestseller block) to the other, then compare revenue per visitor. Clicks and attributed revenue alone overstate value, because many of those shoppers would have bought anyway.

  1. Pick one placement and one primary metric, such as revenue per visitor or average order value.
  2. Define the control honestly: a static bestseller block is a fairer baseline than an empty space.
  3. Split traffic at the visitor level and keep the split stable for the test's duration.
  4. Run for full weekly cycles and avoid launches or sales that hit one group harder.
  5. Check guardrails such as page speed, return rates and margin, not only revenue.

An A/B testing tool handles the split if your engine doesn't, and our list of CRO tools covers the research side.

Can you trust a recommendation engine's own revenue reports?

Treat them as directional. Attributed-revenue reports typically credit orders that included a recommended product or followed a recommendation click, which counts sales the shopper may have made anyway.

What else do buyers ask about product recommendation engines?

Most questions are about data needs and where to start. Short answers follow.

How much data does a recommendation engine need?

Enough interactions for co-viewing and co-purchase patterns to be meaningful. Small stores often get better results from manual complements and bestsellers until volume builds.

Should recommendations be manual or algorithmic?

Use both. Manual picks handle launches, bundles and brand rules, and algorithms handle the long tail of products and shoppers.

Do recommendation widgets slow down product pages?

They can, especially if they load client-side after the page renders. Ask vendors about server-side or edge delivery and measure page speed after installing.

Is a cloud recommendation API cheaper than a Shopify app?

The usage fee may be lower or higher, but the bigger cost is the engineering needed to integrate and maintain it. Compare total cost, not the API bill alone.

What's the verdict on product recommendation engines for ecommerce?

Shopify merchants should exhaust the free Search & Discovery app, then choose Rebuy for order-value offers, LimeSpot for merchandiser-run widgets or Nosto for broader personalization. Engineering-led teams should compare Algolia Recommend, Recombee, Amazon Personalize and Vertex AI Search for commerce, and every buyer should prove lift with a holdout before scaling placements.
