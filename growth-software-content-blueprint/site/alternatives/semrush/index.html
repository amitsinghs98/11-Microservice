Short answer by switch reason

Teams rarely leave an all-in-one suite because every feature disappointed them. They leave because one constraint started to cost money or time. Match the alternative to that constraint:

  • Cost and tracking capacity: SE Ranking, whose Core plan lists more projects and tracked keywords than Semrush's entry plan at a lower monthly price (both checked 4 November 2026). Ubersuggest is a lighter budget option.
  • A simpler workflow: Mangools for small sites and freelancers; Moz Pro for teams that want a familiar, narrower suite.
  • Local SEO depth: BrightLocal, which is built around locations, citations and reviews rather than national keyword research.
  • Backlink specialization: Ahrefs for a broad backlink-led suite; Majestic for link-index analysis alone.
  • Agency reporting: AgencyAnalytics, or SE Ranking with its Agency Pack add-on.

What Semrush does well

Before you cancel, be clear about what you will lose. Semrush covers keyword research, competitor analysis, site audits, position tracking, backlink analysis and content optimization in one account, and its current plans add AI search visibility tracking from the Starter tier. Few alternatives match that breadth.

Its plan cards describe tracked keywords as updated daily, Pro+ adds multi-location and device tracking, and the Pro Report add-on offers branding and white-labeling for client reports. It also has a long record in paid-search and market research. If your team uses three or more of these areas every week, replacing Semrush with a single cheaper tool usually means rebuilding the gaps with several tools. Our workflow comparison of Semrush and Ahrefs shows where the breadth matters most.

Common switching triggers

These triggers come from the plan structure on Semrush's own pricing page, not from reviews or complaints. Checked 4 November 2026:

  • Website and keyword caps: the SEO and Starter plans both list 5 websites and 500 keywords tracked daily. A sixth client or a larger keyword set means Pro+ at $299/mo (15 websites, 1,500 keywords) or a custom Advanced plan.
  • Add-on stacking: extra users start at $45/mo, and client reports are sold as Base Report ($10/mo) and Pro Report ($20/mo) add-ons.
  • Features you do not use: teams that only need rank tracking and audits may be paying for research and AI visibility tools they never open.
  • Specialist depth: local agencies may want more location and review tooling, and link builders may want a link-first index.

Pricing source: Semrush's SEO and AI search pricing page, checked 4 November 2026.

Alternatives grouped by switch reason

For cost and capacity: SE Ranking

Best for: small and mid-sized agencies that track many keywords across many client projects. SE Ranking's pricing page lists Core at $129/mo with 10 projects, 1 manager seat and 2,000 keywords tracked daily, and Growth at $279/mo with 30 projects, 3 seats and 5,000 keywords. Annual billing is listed at a 20% saving (checked 4 November 2026, SE Ranking subscription page).

Trade-off: white-label reporting and client seats come through the Agency Pack, listed from +$69/mo and available on annual billing only. Core also limits historical data to six months. Keyword and backlink databases differ between vendors, so compare results on your own niches during the 14-day free trial the pricing page lists. Our 5-client and 15-client cost model for SE Ranking and Semrush runs the numbers.

Migration note: SE Ranking says it offers free assisted migration on annual plans and self-serve import of projects and ranking history.

For cost on a solo budget: Ubersuggest

Best for: founders and solo marketers who need keyword ideas, a basic site audit and light rank tracking for one or two sites. It is positioned as an approachable, lower-cost tool from Neil Patel's company.

Trade-off: it does not try to match Semrush on competitive research depth, multi-client management or reporting. Skip it if you run an agency or need reliable data for many markets. Check plan limits on the official Ubersuggest site.

For a simpler workflow: Mangools

Best for: freelancers, bloggers and small businesses who find large suites overwhelming. Mangools bundles KWFinder for keyword research with SERP analysis, rank tracking, backlink lookup and site profiling tools under one subscription.

Trade-off: plans are built around daily lookup and tracking allowances that suit a small portfolio, not a 20-client agency. There is no PPC research, and audit tooling is light. Confirm the current lookup limits on the Mangools website.

For a simpler workflow with established metrics: Moz Pro

Best for: in-house teams that want keyword research, link analysis, site crawls and rank tracking without the wider marketing toolkits. Moz's Domain Authority metric is widely recognised by clients and stakeholders.

Trade-off: Moz Pro is narrower than Semrush in content, advertising and market research, and plan limits on tracked keywords and seats should be compared line by line. Moz sells local listings management as a separate product. See Moz Pro on the official site.

For local SEO: BrightLocal

Best for: local agencies and multi-location brands. BrightLocal focuses on local rank tracking, citation building and auditing, review monitoring and Google Business Profile management, with white-label reports for clients.

Trade-off: it does not replace Semrush for national keyword research, competitor gap analysis or backlink work, so many teams keep a research tool alongside it. Pricing scales with locations and services; check the BrightLocal website. Our multi-location local SEO software guide compares it with other local platforms.

For backlink specialization: Ahrefs

Best for: link builders, digital PR teams and content strategists who spend most of their time in competitor link profiles. Ahrefs lists Lite at $129/mo, Standard at $249/mo and Advanced at $449/mo, each with one user included (checked 4 November 2026, Ahrefs pricing page).

Trade-off: the Ahrefs plan table lists Rank Tracker updates as weekly, extra users cost $40 to $80/mo below Enterprise, and its FAQ says it never runs discounts. It is not cheaper than Semrush for most teams; it is a different emphasis. Our guide to link analysis tools covers how to judge link data quality.

For backlink analysis alone: Majestic

Best for: specialists who want a dedicated link index with its own Trust Flow and Citation Flow metrics, and who already have keyword and audit tools.

Trade-off: Majestic is a link intelligence tool, not an SEO suite. You will need separate tools for keywords, rank tracking and audits. Review plans on the Majestic website.

For agency reporting: AgencyAnalytics

Best for: agencies whose main pain is assembling monthly client reports from SEO, analytics, ads and social sources. AgencyAnalytics provides white-labelled dashboards and scheduled reports, and includes some SEO features such as rank tracking.

Trade-off: it is a reporting layer, not a research suite. Most agencies keep a keyword and backlink tool underneath it, so it may add cost rather than replace Semrush. Check how plans scale with client count on the AgencyAnalytics website, and see our client reporting software comparison for other options.

Decision guide

  • You hit the 5-website cap and only need tracking, audits and reports: start with SE Ranking.
  • You are a solo operator who opens Semrush twice a month: try Mangools or Ubersuggest, and fill gaps with free SEO tools such as Search Console.
  • Most of your clients are local businesses: BrightLocal plus a light research tool.
  • Links are the billable work: Ahrefs, or Majestic beside a cheaper suite.
  • Reporting takes more hours than research: AgencyAnalytics, or SE Ranking's Agency Pack.
  • You use keyword research, PPC data and audits every week: stay on Semrush and review your add-ons instead.

Migration and data-export questions

Ask these before you sign anything new, and before the Semrush renewal date passes:

  1. Can I export my tracked keyword lists, tags and ranking history from Semrush in a format the new tool can import?
  2. Does the new vendor import ranking history, or does tracking start from zero on day one?
  3. Will client report templates need rebuilding, and who on the team owns that work?
  4. Which metrics will change? Authority scores, traffic estimates and volumes differ between vendors, so clients need a note.
  5. Are user seats, client logins and white-label features included, or sold as add-ons?
  6. Is migration help tied to annual billing, as SE Ranking states for its assisted migration?
  7. What are the cancellation and refund terms if the switch fails?

If you are switching to cut overall spend, a wider review of overlapping subscriptions often finds more savings; our guide to auditing a marketing stack sets out the process. FAQs

What is the cheapest real alternative to Semrush for an agency?

Of the tools whose pricing we could verify, SE Ranking Core lists 10 projects and 2,000 daily-tracked keywords at $129/mo, against 5 websites and 500 keywords on Semrush's $139/mo SEO plan (checked 4 November 2026). Data depth and reporting add-ons still need comparing.

Is there a free alternative to Semrush?

Not a complete one. Google Search Console, Ahrefs Free for site owners and Semrush's own free plan cover parts of the job, but none replaces a paid suite for multi-client work.

Will I lose my ranking history if I switch?

You keep it only if you export it first. Some vendors, including SE Ranking, describe import options for ranking history; confirm the format before cancelling.

Should I switch or downgrade?

If the problem is a single add-on or a seat count, review your Semrush configuration first. Switching makes more sense when a structural limit, such as the website cap, keeps forcing a higher tier.

Conclusion

Semrush remains a strong default for teams that use its breadth. The case for an alternative is clearest when one constraint dominates: tracking capacity for agencies, simplicity for solo operators, location tooling for local work, link depth for link builders, or reporting time for client teams. Pick the alternative that solves that constraint, run its trial on real client data, and move your history before the renewal date.
