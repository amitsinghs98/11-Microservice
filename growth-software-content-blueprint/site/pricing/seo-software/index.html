Quick answer

Based on list prices checked on 4 November 2026, a five-client agency should budget roughly $145 to $310 a month for an SEO suite with a second user. A ten-client agency with four people lands at about $195 to $530. At twenty clients and eight people, one of the three suites we modelled moves to a custom quote and the other two range from about $490 to $1,110 a month. That spread comes mostly from seats, report limits and rank-tracking frequency, not from the base plan.

The cheapest figure is not automatically the best value. The suites differ in data depth, update frequency and interface, and our workflow comparison of Semrush and Ahrefs covers those differences. This page covers cost only. For product recommendations, start with our SEO software buying guide by team type or the broader SEO tools hub.

How SEO software is priced

  • Tiered suites with usage caps. Semrush, Ahrefs and SE Ranking sell plans that bundle projects (or monitored websites), tracked keywords, crawl budgets and report rows. Extra users are charged separately.
  • Per-client or per-campaign reporting. Client reporting platforms such as AgencyAnalytics generally scale with the number of clients connected. See the AgencyAnalytics pricing page for current tiers.
  • Per-location local tools. Local SEO platforms such as BrightLocal price by location or location bundles. Grid rank trackers such as Local Falcon typically use scan credits.
  • Per-licence desktop software. Crawlers like Screaming Frog SEO Spider sell annual licences per user.
  • Quote-only enterprise tiers. Every major suite has one, usually with an annual commitment.

Monthly versus annual billing

Semrush advertises savings of up to 17% on annual billing. Ahrefs also says annual billing saves up to 17%, and its FAQ says you switch to annual by contacting support. SE Ranking advertises 20% off for annual plans, which are billed upfront. Its FAQ says sales can discuss alternatives such as quarterly payments on an annual commitment. All checked 4 November 2026.

Annual billing has a hidden effect for agencies: SE Ranking's white-label Agency Pack is listed as annual billing only, so the cheapest white-label setup there requires an annual commitment. Ahrefs Enterprise also requires one.

Limits and add-ons that change the bill

  • Seats. Semrush lists additional users starting at $45 per month. Ahrefs charges $40, $60 or $80 per extra user depending on plan, with caps of 2, 5 or 10 extra users. SE Ranking lists extra manager seats from $16.
  • White-label reports. Semrush's Pro Report add-on, listed at $20 per month, includes branding and white-labelling. Its Base Report add-on is $10 per month. SE Ranking's Agency Pack is +$69 per month, billed annually, and includes white label for the platform and reporting plus 30 client seats.
  • Report counts. Ahrefs Report Builder includes 5 reports on Lite and Standard and 15 on Advanced. The Report Builder add-on is $99 per month for 50 reports. SE Ranking includes 10 reports on Core and 30 on Growth.
  • Usage credits. Ahrefs Lite includes 1,000 credits per user per month, while higher plans list unlimited credits. A research-heavy agency can hit that ceiling on Lite.
  • Local locations. SE Ranking includes 3 local locations, with extra locations from $18.40, and a Local and Map Rank Tracker allowance of 1,500 keywords.
  • Tracking frequency. Ahrefs Rank Tracker updates weekly on every plan. Semrush and SE Ranking list daily tracking. If clients expect daily movement, weekly data is a product limitation, not just a cost issue.

All add-on figures were checked 4 November 2026 on the vendor pages linked above.

Three agency scenarios

These are hypothetical profiles built to show how limits interact. Assumptions: about 100 tracked keywords per client; one monthly report per client; extra users bought at the lowest listed rate; and add-on prices assumed unchanged on annual billing unless a vendor says otherwise. Local and reporting tools beyond the suite are excluded and covered separately below. "From" means the vendor lists a starting price.

Profile 1: five clients, two people

Needs: 5 sites, 500 keywords, 2 users, white-label PDFs and 2 local clients.

  • Semrush: the SEO plan fits the limits exactly ($139), but Starter's fuller keyword tools are the realistic choice. Starter $199 + 1 user from $45 + Pro Report $20 = from $264 a month. Confirm with Semrush that add-on users are available on your chosen plan.
  • Ahrefs: Lite $129 + 1 user at $40 = $169 a month, with weekly tracking and 5 Report Builder reports. If 1,000 credits per user proves tight, Standard $249 + 1 user at $60 = $309.
  • SE Ranking: Core $129 + 1 seat from $16 = from $145 monthly, without white label. With the annual-only Agency Pack: $103.20 + $16 + $69 = from $188.20 a month, billed annually.

Profile 2: ten clients, four people

Needs: 10 sites, 1,500 keywords, 4 users, 10 white-label reports and 4 local clients.

  • Semrush: Pro+ $299 + 3 users from $45 ($135) + Pro Report $20 = from $454 a month, or from $403.17 with annual base pricing.
  • Ahrefs: Standard $249 + 3 users at $60 ($180) = $429. Ten reports exceed the 5 included, so the Report Builder add-on at $99 brings it to $528 a month.
  • SE Ranking: Core covers exactly 10 projects and 10 reports with no headroom. Core $129 + 3 seats from $16 ($48) + 1 extra location from $18.40 = from $195.40 monthly. Adding white label annually: $103.20 + $48 + $18.40 + $69 = from $238.60 a month.

Profile 3: twenty clients, eight people

Needs: 20 sites, 4,000 keywords, 8 users, 20 reports and 10 local clients.

  • Semrush: 20 sites exceeds Pro+'s 15, so the Advanced plan applies. It's custom-priced, so request a quote.
  • Ahrefs: Advanced $449 + 7 users at $80 ($560) = $1,009. Twenty reports exceed the 15 included, so adding Report Builder brings it to $1,108 a month.
  • SE Ranking: Growth $279 + 5 seats from $16 ($80) + 7 extra locations from $18.40 ($128.80) = from $487.80 monthly. With annual billing and the Agency Pack: $223.20 + $80 + $128.80 + $69 = from $501 a month.

Free plans versus trials

Semrush lists a free account with 1 demo project and 10 reports per day, and its paid plans show a "Try for free" option. The page we checked didn't state the trial length. Ahrefs says it never runs discounts. Instead, it offers Ahrefs Free for website owners and a Starter plan at $29 per month. SE Ranking offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card. Its FAQ says the trial includes up to 10 projects and 750 tracked keywords per day. All checked 4 November 2026. For the no-cost tools worth keeping alongside a suite, see our free SEO tools for small agencies.

Renewal and next-tier triggers

  • Semrush: the step from 5 to 15 websites, then to Advanced at 40+, is the main trigger. The sixth client forces an upgrade from Starter to Pro+.
  • Ahrefs: extra-user caps of 2, 5 and 10 force a plan change as headcount grows, as does the unverified-project limit for prospects and competitors.
  • SE Ranking: the eleventh project or report pushes Core to Growth. Keyword overages are sold separately, with extra keywords listed from $22.40 on Core and from $32 on Growth.

On renewal, check whether your annual price was promotional and whether the plan you're on is still sold. If not, ask whether you keep your current limits.

Questions to ask sales

  • Which limits are per account and which are per user or per project?
  • Are add-on users and report add-ons priced the same on annual billing?
  • What happens to rank history and reports if we downgrade?
  • Can client-facing viewers access reports without a paid seat?
  • Are overages billed automatically? Ahrefs' FAQ says pay-as-you-go usage is charged automatically once enabled.
