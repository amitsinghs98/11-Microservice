Quick answer

In our hypothetical small-team model, licences are less than half of first-year CRM cost once setup and ongoing admin time are counted. In our hypothetical 5-seat model below, annual licences on mid-tier plans range from about $1,400 to $2,200, while assumed admin and setup time adds roughly $3,400.

The biggest swings come from three things: seat caps that force a tier jump, usage allowances such as credits or contacts, and quote-only items such as implementation and premium support. Compare those before you compare seat prices. If you are still choosing a product, start with our small-business CRM shortlist.

How CRMs charge

  • Per seat, per month. The common model, used by Zoho CRM, Capsule, HubSpot and Attio. Cost scales linearly with headcount.
  • Flat rate per organization. Bitrix24 charges per organization, with a set number of users included in each tier. Cheap per head, but you pay for the whole bundle.
  • Seats plus usage. Attio adds seat and workspace credits for AI and workflow features; HubSpot's Starter bundle lists HubSpot Credits. Usage can become the variable cost.
  • Quote-only. Enterprise tiers, implementation services and premium support are often priced by sales, which is where budgets drift.
  • Open source. No licence fee, but hosting and admin become the whole bill. Our free CRM comparison for startups covers this route.

Monthly versus annual billing

Annual billing typically saves money but locks seats in. Capsule's Growth plan is $36 per user per month billed annually versus $40 billed monthly. Attio states a 20% discount on Plus and Pro for annual billing. Bitrix24 Basic is $49 per month billed annually (one payment of $588 a year) versus $69 billed monthly. All checked 4 November 2026.

Pay monthly while headcount is uncertain or you are still validating fit. Switch to annual once seat count is stable for two quarters, and ask whether seats can be reduced mid-term. Zoho's pricing page states customers are not bound by contracts and can pay monthly or yearly.

Limits, add-ons and onboarding

Usage allowances

Attio lists 250, 1,500 and 10,000 workspace credits per month on Free, Plus and Pro, and sells 5,000 extra workspace credits for $85 a month, or $70 a month on annual billing (checked 4 November 2026). Capsule caps contacts at 30,000 on Starter, 60,000 on Growth and 120,000 on Advanced. HubSpot's Starter card lists 500 HubSpot Credits and 1,000 marketing contacts for Marketing Hub Starter.

Seat caps that force tier jumps

Attio Plus allows up to 10 seats, so an eleventh user moves you to Pro. Bitrix24 tiers include fixed user counts. Plan your 12-month headcount before choosing a tier.

Onboarding and support

Capsule's pricing page states it charges no setup or onboarding fee. Zoho offers paid support plans and guided onboarding through its Jumpstart programme, priced separately. Based on our editorial knowledge, HubSpot has charged one-time onboarding fees on some Professional and Enterprise subscriptions, and Salesforce implementations are often delivered by partners on quote; confirm both directly.

Integrations and admin time

Budget for middleware, enrichment and scheduling tools the CRM does not include, and for someone to maintain fields, workflows and permissions. That admin time is a real cost even when nobody invoices it.

Free plan versus trial

Free plans (Zoho, Capsule, Attio, HubSpot and Bitrix24 all list one) suit evaluation with real data but carry caps. Trials unlock paid features for a fixed window: Capsule and Attio each list 14-day trials (checked 4 November 2026). Run your trial against one real pipeline, and check what happens to data when it ends.

Renewal and next-tier triggers

Common triggers for a price rise: a new hire crossing a seat cap, needing a second pipeline, role-based permissions, contacts above the plan limit, credits running out, or a first-year promotion ending. HubSpot was advertising discounted Starter pricing for new customers when we checked, so ask for the renewal rate in writing.

Annual renewal checklist

  1. Count active users over the last 90 days and remove idle seats.
  2. Compare contacts, records and credits used against plan limits.
  3. List features you pay for but have not used.
  4. Total admin hours spent on the CRM this year.
  5. Request the renewal quote 60 days before the term ends.
  6. Check whether integrations or add-ons renew on separate dates.

A wider subscription review helps too; our marketing stack audit method shows how to find duplicate tools.

Questions to ask sales

  • Is there a seat minimum, and can seats be reduced mid-term?
  • Which features require a separate hub, add-on or higher tier?
  • Is onboarding mandatory, and what does it include?
  • Which support level is included, and what does premium support cost?
  • Is the quoted price a promotion, and what is the renewal price?
  • What are the API, storage, contact and credit limits?
  • Can we export all data, including activities, if we leave?

FAQs

What does a CRM cost per user?

Among the plans used in our scenarios, licences ranged from $23 (Zoho Professional) to $79 (Attio Pro, annual) per user per month, based on prices checked 4 November 2026. Flat-rate tools such as Bitrix24 are far cheaper per head.

Is annual billing worth it?

Once headcount is stable. Capsule and Attio both price annual billing lower, but you commit to seats for the year.

How should I budget admin time?

Estimate hours a month for field changes, workflow fixes, user changes and reporting, then multiply by a loaded hourly cost. Our scenarios used 3 hours for 5 seats and 10 hours for 15 seats as illustrations. For agency-specific setup, see how an agency should evaluate a CRM.
