Which Mailchimp alternative fits your reason for switching?

If cost at list size is the trigger, look first at MailerLite or Brevo. If you run a store, look at Klaviyo or Omnisend; if automation depth is the gap, ActiveCampaign; and if you publish a newsletter or sell digital products, Kit.

  • Large list, modest budget: MailerLite for a simple contact-based alternative, or Brevo if you'd rather pay for email volume than for stored contacts.
  • Shopify or other online store: Klaviyo for deep event data and segmentation, Omnisend for email and SMS with a lighter learning curve.
  • B2B or lifecycle automation: ActiveCampaign, whose visual automation builder is the center of every plan.
  • Creator or consultant newsletter: Kit, built around subscribers, tags and selling to an audience.

What does Mailchimp do well that you might lose?

Mailchimp is easy to start with, widely integrated and familiar to freelancers and agencies, which lowers handover friction. Its templates, landing pages and broad app ecosystem cover most small-business campaign needs without extra tools.

That familiarity is a real asset. If a contractor or new hire can run your campaigns on day one, you save onboarding time that a more specialized tool would cost. Before switching, list the Mailchimp features you actually use each month, so you compare alternatives against your real workload and not the full feature grid on the Mailchimp pricing page.

Why do growing businesses switch away from Mailchimp?

The most common triggers are billing that rises with list size, automation needs that outgrow the plan you're on, and ecommerce segmentation that needs richer store data. None of these mean Mailchimp is a poor product; they mean your requirements have changed.

  • How contacts are counted. Mailchimp's help center explains which contact types count toward your plan, and unsubscribed contacts are among those that can. Archiving inactive contacts lowers the count, but many teams only discover this when the bill jumps.
  • Automation gating. Multi-step customer journeys and advanced segmentation scale with plan tier, so a team that adds branches and conditions can be pushed up a tier for one feature.
  • Ecommerce depth. Stores that want predictive segments, browse-abandonment flows and product-level personalization often find dedicated ecommerce platforms go further.
  • B2B handoff. Teams that need lead scoring and a sales pipeline next to email start looking at automation-first tools.

Which alternatives cost less as your list grows?

For a large list you email often, a contact-tier tool with lower per-contact pricing such as MailerLite is usually the cheaper switch. For a large list you email rarely, a volume-based tool like Brevo can cost less, because you pay for sends rather than for every stored contact.

MailerLite: best for a straightforward, lower-cost switch

MailerLite covers the campaign, automation, landing page and form features most Mailchimp users rely on, with an editor that's quick to learn. It's a good fit for teams that want the same kind of tool at a lower list-size cost. Skip it if you need deep ecommerce segmentation or a built-in sales pipeline.

Brevo: best for big, infrequently mailed lists

Brevo bundles marketing email, transactional email, SMS and a basic CRM, and its plans are built around how much you send. That suits businesses with a large but quiet list, or SaaS teams that want marketing and transactional sending in one account. Consider another option if you send several campaigns a week to the whole list, which erodes the volume advantage.

Kit: best for newsletters and creator businesses

Kit organizes everything around subscribers and tags, with tools for selling digital products and growing through recommendations. It's a good fit for consultants and creators whose list is the business. Skip it if your emails need heavy visual design or store-level product blocks. Which Mailchimp alternatives suit ecommerce stores?

Klaviyo is the deeper choice when segmentation by purchase, browse and predicted behavior drives revenue. Omnisend is the simpler choice for smaller stores that want email and SMS together without a heavy setup.

Klaviyo: best for data-heavy ecommerce programs

Klaviyo syncs store events into profiles and uses them for flows, segments and predictive analytics. Its billing is based on active profiles, with SMS paid separately; check the current tiers on the Klaviyo pricing page. Skip it if your list is large and your average order value is low, because cost rises quickly with profiles. Our Klaviyo alternatives guide covers the brands that outgrow it in the other direction.

Omnisend: best for smaller stores adding SMS

Omnisend combines email, SMS and prebuilt ecommerce automations such as cart and browse abandonment. It's a good fit for Shopify and WooCommerce stores that want ready-made flows. Consider another option if you need advanced predictive segments or a data team's level of reporting. The wider field is in our ecommerce email platform shortlist.

Which alternatives go deeper on automation?

ActiveCampaign is the clearest step up in automation depth, with branching, goals, lead scoring and a deals pipeline in one account. It suits B2B and service businesses that need email to hand leads to sales.

ActiveCampaign: best for lifecycle and B2B automation

ActiveCampaign's visual automation builder is the core of the product, so complex nurtures don't require a premium add-on. A good fit for teams that think in triggers and conditions. Skip it if you only send a monthly newsletter, because you'll pay for depth you won't use. How do you migrate off Mailchimp without losing deliverability?

Export every audience with consent status, tags and merge fields, import unsubscribed and cleaned contacts as suppressions, then rebuild automations and authenticate your domain on the new platform before the first send. Plan for a few weeks of overlap.

  1. Audit first. Archive or delete contacts you won't migrate, and note which automations still earn their place.
  2. Export everything. Subscribed, unsubscribed and cleaned contacts, tags, merge fields and signup dates. Unsubscribes must arrive as suppressions, not as fresh subscribers.
  3. Authenticate. Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your sending domain on the new platform.
  4. Rebuild automations. Customer journeys don't transfer between vendors, so recreate the welcome, abandonment and re-engagement flows by hand and test each branch.
  5. Replace forms and integrations. Swap embed codes on every page and reconnect store, CRM and webinar integrations.
  6. Warm up. Send to your most engaged segment first and widen over several sends while watching bounces and complaints. Our email deliverability tools comparison lists independent monitors.
  7. Check the contract. Confirm when your Mailchimp billing renews, so you don't pay for two platforms longer than needed.

Who should stay on Mailchimp?

Stay if your list is small and tidy, you send straightforward campaigns, and contractors or new hires already know the tool. Switching costs time and some deliverability risk, so it only pays when an alternative solves a problem you actually have.

Archiving inactive contacts and trimming unused features can also fix a Mailchimp bill without a migration. Try that first, and compare the result with quotes from two alternatives.

What else do buyers ask about Mailchimp alternatives?

The usual follow-ups cover free plans, ecommerce fit and how long a switch takes. Short answers follow.

What is the cheapest Mailchimp alternative?

It depends on your list size and send frequency. Contact-tier tools such as MailerLite suit frequent sending, while volume-based Brevo suits large lists mailed rarely. Price both at your real numbers.

Is Klaviyo a good Mailchimp alternative for Shopify stores?

Yes, if you'll use its store event data for flows and segments. Smaller stores that mainly want ready-made flows and SMS may find Omnisend simpler.

How long does a Mailchimp migration take?

A small list with a few automations can move in a week or two, including a gradual warm-up. Accounts with many journeys and integrations need longer, mainly to rebuild and test automations.

Will my unsubscribes carry over?

Only if you export them and import them as suppressions. Never import unsubscribed contacts as active subscribers on the new platform.

What's the verdict on Mailchimp alternatives?

In our editorial assessment, MailerLite is the easiest like-for-like switch on cost, Brevo suits large, quiet lists, Klaviyo and Omnisend serve stores, ActiveCampaign serves automation-heavy teams and Kit serves newsletter businesses. Pick the one that fixes your specific reason for leaving, and stay put if no reason is strong enough.
